BLENDER / CREATIVE WORLD-BUILDING

Midnight City in 3D

A fictional future New York, developed through maps, 3D scenes, and lighting studies.

From a map to a place

Creative direction

My Midnight City world provides the setting and visual direction. The Blender scenes and rendering workflow were developed with AI assistance.

The process

The scene translates a stylized map into coastlines, buildings, transport routes, and landmarks. Later passes add facade lighting, roof details, labels, and views beneath the surface.

Case notes

Keep a large scene readable

The overview establishes the whole region. Separate detail views make individual buildings and transport connections easier to see.

Separate setting from geography

Coastlines and positions are approximate. Future transit, craters, and buildings belong to the fictional setting; this is not a surveyed model of present-day New York.
